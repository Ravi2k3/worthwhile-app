The cooldown

A price in hours is a party trick. A timer that comes back and asks again is a habit.

1

See it in work time

Type an amount and the answer appears. A coffee reads in minutes. A television reads in weeks. Checking costs nothing and asks for nothing.

2

Put it behind a wait

Ten minutes to a month, or park it on the wishlist with no clock at all. Very few impulses survive a wait.

3

Still want it?

When the time is up, one question arrives. Buy it, give it longer, or drop it and keep the money and the hours it stood for.

Checking costs nothing

38 currencies, each its own way

Digits group the way your own region groups them, so fifteen lakh comes out as 15,00,000 and reads at a glance.

Reminders that arrive on time

The question arrives at the moment you picked. And when your phone is going to hold it back, the app warns you first, so a late reminder is never a surprise.

History, both halves

Kept and spent side by side, grouped by day, month or year, searchable. Your own decisions are yours to look at.

A wishlist with no clock
